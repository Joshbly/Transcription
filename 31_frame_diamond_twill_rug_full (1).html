Thread-level detail
Every line is one thread. Weft passes over 3 warps, under 1. Shadows fall where threads dip under their crossing neighbor. Zoom in (Ctrl/Cmd-scroll) — every thread stays crisp.
The loom this requires
31 frames stacked vertically, each controlling every 31st warp thread. Requires a computerized dobby head — no human can operate 31 foot treadles. AVL and ARM Loom make dobby looms in this range.
